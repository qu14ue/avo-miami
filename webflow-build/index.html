Why seed oil free?

Canola, sunflower and soybean oils are cheap, heavily refined and used in almost every restaurant kitchen in Miami. We cook exclusively with olive oil instead — in the dressings, on the plancha and in the fryer — so a bowl at Avo tastes like the ingredients it is made of, not like the oil it was cooked in. No seed oils, no microwave, no processed shortcuts. Read more on our blog

Frequently asked questions

Is Avo Miami seed oil free?

Yes. Every dish at Avo Miami is cooked with olive oil only. We never use canola, sunflower, soybean or any other seed oil, in any preparation.

Where is Avo Miami located?

Avo Miami has three locations: Miami Beach (1834 Bay Rd, Miami Beach, FL 33139), Coconut Grove (2911 Grand Ave, Miami, FL 33133) and South Miami (5958 S Dixie Hwy, South Miami, FL 33143).

What are Avo Miami’s opening hours?

All three Avo Miami locations are open daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Does Avo Miami deliver?

Yes. You can order delivery or pickup from any Avo Miami location at order.avo-miami.com or through the Avo Miami app, available on the App Store and Google Play.

Are the meals designed by a nutritionist?

Yes. The Avo Miami menu is dietitian-designed and guided by nutritionists, built around organic vegetables, grass-fed meats and wild-caught proteins for a balanced macronutrient profile.

Does Avo Miami have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes. The menu has dedicated vegan and vegetarian sections and a gluten-free section, and those options are also available on the catering menu.

Does Avo Miami offer catering?

Yes. Avo Miami caters nutrient-dense, chef-crafted platters portioned for groups of 10, for corporate luncheons and private events. Catering is ordered online and can be scheduled for a specific date or delivery.

How does the Avo Miami rewards program work?

You earn points on every purchase made through the Avo Miami app and redeem them for menu items: 100 points for coffee, teas or matcha blends, 150 for baked items, 175 for cold-pressed juices, 200 for signature toasts, 250 for protein smoothies, 275 for salads, 500 for a salmon, tuna or shrimp bowl and 700 for a filet mignon or lamb chops bowl.
